## Surface

Scope: Redesign the existing SpecUse landing page; preserve its section structure and distinguish planned features from what is currently available.
Visitor mode: Persuade.

## Audience and action

Thai gamers and creators, often visiting on phones and sharing PC-build ideas in LINE or Facebook. Help them understand the planned approach to suitable, compatible, budget-aware PC builds. The primary landing-page action is to explore a sample build pathway; the build editor and the other product features are not implemented yet.

## Proof and content

Show the planned product flow through the component-selection concept preview, planned benefits, planned Guest/Member differences, and sample build pathways. State that the build editor, compatibility checks, catalog/prices, accounts, saved builds, and sharing are not currently available. Label all sample content as illustrative; do not imply real popularity, member submissions, reviews, prices, or a functioning build editor.

## Constraints

Keep the current section structure and Thai user-facing content, changing wording only to identify planned features and the prototype's current limits. Preserve the mobile-first layout and accessible controls. Replace the former dark navy/teal world with the user-pinned dark-red world: obsidian/maroon ground, restrained red grid/mesh, multi-tone ruby/crimson accents, translucent burgundy glass surfaces, crisp high-contrast type, and ruby primary actions. The main CTA scrolls to sample build pathways. Until a sign-in route exists, navigation to the Member information section must use a descriptive information label, not a sign-in label. Do not invent catalog proof or claims.

## Direction contract

THESIS: SpecUse makes PC planning feel like a clear route from intended use and budget to a build worth evaluating, refusing generic gaming hype and unearned popularity claims.

OWN-WORLD: Obsidian red-black fields, a quiet crimson digital grid, layered glass in burgundy, hairline ruby edges, white Thai typography, and a multi-tone crimson spectrum for active states and actions. Depth comes from translucent material and soft red light, never from teal or unrelated accent colors.

STORY: A Thai gamer or creator sees that SpecUse is in development, learns the planned budget and compatibility capabilities and planned Guest/Member entitlements, then explores clearly labeled sample pathways. Samples are examples, not real or popular member builds.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Keep the sticky compact header. On desktop, set the exact two-line Thai headline and concise development-status copy at left, with the clearly labeled, non-functional component concept preview at right; place the ruby sample-pathway action before the secondary planned-workflow action. On mobile, stack this content in the same reading order with full-width touch-friendly actions and no horizontal overflow.

FORM: User-pinned dark-red minimalist gaming interface, preserving the existing landing-page section structure.
